# Project conventions

- Share the seed collection's product data and bilingual content between the home section and dedicated localized collection pages to prevent conflicting prices and copy.
- Collection pages use separate Hungarian and Serbian URLs with self-referencing canonicals and reciprocal hreflang; route language takes precedence over saved language for crawlable localized content.
- Use the existing contact channels for product inquiries; the site is not a checkout.
- Reuse actual gallery photography; the fenugreek image is a crop of its package in the existing family photograph, not generated packaging.
