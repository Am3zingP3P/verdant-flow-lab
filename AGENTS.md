# Project conventions

- Share the seed collection's product data and bilingual content between the home section and dedicated localized collection pages to prevent conflicting prices and copy.
- Keep dedicated collection opening copy in separate localized fields from home promotion copy so opening variants do not change the home page.
- Render the home collection as a lightweight promotion linking to the localized full collection; keep product selection in the standalone view to avoid duplicating the full browsing experience.
- Collection pages use separate Hungarian and Serbian URLs with self-referencing canonicals and reciprocal hreflang; route language takes precedence over saved language for crawlable localized content.
- Use the existing contact channels for product inquiries; the site is not a checkout.
- Reuse actual gallery photography; product data may have a null image rendered as a localized photo placeholder until an authentic suitable photo is available, avoiding misleading packaging.
- Scope collection readability overrides with a class applied to both localized collection pages, not shared page chrome or the global typography, to preserve other routes.
